Your Recycling Guide

Recycling properly keeps materials out of landfills. Check our table below for common items.

Material Recyclable? Methods
Plastic Bottles Yes Rinse and sort by number (e.g., PET #1)
Paper Yes Flatten boxes; remove tape
Glass Jars Yes Remove lids; rinse thoroughly
Aluminum Cans Yes Crush to save space
Styrofoam No (in most areas) Seek special drop-off programs
