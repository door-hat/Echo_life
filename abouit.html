What is Sustainability?

Sustainability is the practice of using resources in a way that meets our current needs without compromising the ability of future generations to meet theirs. It's about balance—environmental, social, and economic.

Why It Matters

Our planet faces challenges like climate change, deforestation, and pollution. Adopting sustainable habits reduces waste, conserves energy, and protects biodiversity. By choosing sustainability, we create a healthier world for all.
