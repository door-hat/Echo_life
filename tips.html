Practical Eco-Friendly Living Tips

Start small and build habits that last. Here are 10 tips to get you going!

Daily Habits

  1. Switch to reusable bags and bottles to cut down on plastic waste.
  2. Turn off lights and unplug devices when not in use to save energy.
  3. Opt for public transport or biking instead of driving alone.

At Home

  • Use energy-efficient bulbs and appliances.
  • Compost food scraps to reduce landfill waste.
    • Include veggie peels and coffee grounds.
    • Avoid meat or dairy in compost.
  • Choose natural cleaning products over chemical ones.

Shopping & Eating

  • Buy local and seasonal produce to lower your carbon footprint.
  • Reduce meat consumption—try one meat-free day a week.
  • Repair or donate items instead of throwing them away.
